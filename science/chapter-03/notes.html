1
Making an LED Glow

Take two electric cells, an LED of any colour, a cell holder that can fit two cells, and two lengths of electric wire. The LED glows only when its longer (positive) wire connects to the battery’s positive terminal and its shorter (negative) wire connects to the negative terminal.

  • Insert two cells in the holder with each negative terminal towards the spring side
  • Connect the battery’s positive wire to the LED’s longer wire, and the negative wire to its shorter wire
  • The LED glows in one direction only — reversing the wires stops it from glowing
